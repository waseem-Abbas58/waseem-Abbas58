### 🚀 About me

- 📱 **Flutter developer** building production apps for Android and iOS: smooth animations, offline support, dark mode, multiple languages and RTL.
- 🤖 **AI apps:** plant identification, health checks, chatbots and voice assistants with Gemini and other AI APIs.
- 🌐 **Full-stack:** Node.js and Express APIs, MongoDB and Firebase, admin panels and websites with Next.js.
- 🤝 I work with clients worldwide and take projects from the first sketch to the store.
